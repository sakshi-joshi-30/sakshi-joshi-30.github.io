**Duration:** Jan 2026 – Present | *Ongoing*

Building a **large-scale multilingual child speech corpus** for oral reading fluency across Indian languages, capturing grade-level reading tasks and detailed reading errors.

Designing **AudioLLM-based models** to jointly assess accuracy, fluency, and prosody, along with evaluation pipelines using WER, PER, and miscue-based metrics for scalable literacy assessment.
