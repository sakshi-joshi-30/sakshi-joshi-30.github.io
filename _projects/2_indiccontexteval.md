**Duration:** Aug 2025 – Present | *Under Review (Interspeech 2026)*

Developed a **56-hour multilingual benchmark** spanning 8 Indian languages and 23 domains to evaluate contextual grounding in AudioLLMs under controlled prompting settings.

Designed a **7-level prompting framework** to systematically analyze how models utilise domain metadata, natural language descriptions, and entity-level context, revealing significant variation in contextual reasoning.
