**Duration:** Jul 2024 – Aug 2025

Built **SRUTI**, a benchmark dataset of rural Bhojpuri women's speech across domains such as healthcare, agriculture, governance, and finance, highlighting severe performance gaps in existing ASR systems.

Proposed a scalable synthetic data generation pipeline using just 25–30 seconds of speech per speaker to create 100+ hours of data, improving ASR performance by 4.7 WER in low-resource settings.
