I am a PhD Research Scholar in the [Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence](https://dsai.iitm.ac.in/) at **IIT Madras**, advised by [Prof. Mitesh M. Khapra](https://www.cse.iitm.ac.in/~miteshk/). I am affiliated with [AI4Bharat](https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/), where my research focuses on **Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) for low-resource domains and underrepresented demographics**.

I build datasets, benchmarks, and models aimed at making speech technology work for communities that existing systems underserve — spanning diverse accents, dialects, and low-resource settings in India. My current interests include:

- **Low-resource & domain-specific ASR** for underrepresented populations
- **Benchmark creation** for evaluating ASR across accents and dialects
- **Child speech recognition** for educational settings

At Interspeech 2025, I presented our work on ASR for rural Bhojpuri women and received the **Best Theme Paper Award**. I was also selected to attend the **Google DeepMind Research Symposium 2025**.

Before my PhD, I was a Project Associate at AI4Bharat (Jan–Jul 2023), contributing to data collection and evaluation for multilingual ASR projects including LAHAJA, Svarah, and IndicVoices. I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from Medicaps University, Indore (2021).
