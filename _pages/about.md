I am a third year PhD student in the [Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence](https://dsai.iitm.ac.in/) at IIT Madras, advised by [Prof. Mitesh M. Khapra](https://www.cse.iitm.ac.in/~miteshk/). My research focuses on building inclusive speech technologies for underserved languages, domains, and demographic groups in India.

I work on automatic speech recognition (ASR), child speech recognition, dialect-robust speech systems, and benchmark creation for inclusive speech technologies. My current research explores automated Oral Reading Fluency (ORF) assessment for children, conversational ASR for educational settings, and robust speech recognition for Hindi dialects through targeted data collection, synthetic data generation, and evaluation.

Before joining the PhD program, I worked as a Project Associate at [AI4Bharat](https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/) (Jan–Jul 2023), contributing to multilingual ASR projects including LAHAJA, Svarah, and IndicVoices. I also worked at [IIT Madras Research Park](https://respark.iitm.ac.in/) under [Prof. Ashok Jhunjhunwala](https://www.ee.iitm.ac.in/~ashok/) on a [Uniphore](https://www.uniphore.com/) speech project for six months. I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from Medicaps University, Indore (2021).
